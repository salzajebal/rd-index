---
name: VORA hero design direction
description: Approved visual direction for the VORA Markets landing hero.
---

The landing hero uses a full-screen typography composition rather than an enclosed hero card: oversized type, sparse grid lines, and small terminal-style metadata carry the visual hierarchy.

**Why:** The enclosed gold-bordered hero felt dated and visually separate from the product; the typography-led layout better conveys a modern trading platform.

**How to apply:** Keep future hero refinements minimal and sharp-edged. Avoid large enclosing boxes, rounded cards, or decorative illustrations that compete with the headline.
